Published

Oral · IEEE ISBI 2026 First author

AI Generalisation Gap in Comorbid Sleep Disorder Staging

Saswata Bose, Suvadeep Maiti, Shivam Kumar Sharma, Mythirayee S, Tapabrata Chakraborti, Srijitesh PR, Raju S. Bapi

Automated sleep staging works well on the cohorts it was trained on. Real patients rarely resemble those cohorts — they arrive with a stroke, an obstructive apnoea, a second disorder sitting on top of the first. This paper measures how far the models fall in that setting, and pairs the drop with explanations a clinician can inspect. Work with NIMHANS Bangalore and UCL London.
